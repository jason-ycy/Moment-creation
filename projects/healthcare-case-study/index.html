Demand for age care

Demand across the Australian healthcare landscape is growing faster than workforce supply, driven by an ageing population and increasingly complex care needs. Additional pressures come in full flight as the government invests more money into this space.

Platform fragmentation and legacy sprawl

Products live on different technology stacks, to massive unstructured datasets that continue to expand across different domains such as primary care, aged care, and hospital care. There is a need for more data-driven orchestration, consolidation and rationalisation.

Regulatory overhead and compliance burdens

The Aged Care Act 2024 came into effect Nov 2025, with new regulatory needs to be met around quality, safety and privacy. The challenge here is managing conformance pressures across existing and new product suites.

Competitive pressure for AI enabled tools

AI is the biggest conversation in industry today. Rising demand to stay competitive for AI-enabled tools that reduce admin burden and improve productivity, allowing healthcare workers to dedicate more time to empathetic communication, deeper patient engagement, and the delivery of personalised care.
