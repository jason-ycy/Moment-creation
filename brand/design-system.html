01 · Brand identity

A shared purpose

Cognizant Moment is a sub-brand of Cognizant. We partner with leading clients to differentiate, identify opportunities, and build customer engagement from conception to delivery. We use intelligent experience orchestration to connect data, technology, and operations across entire ecosystems — harnessing AI and personalization to delight customers and grow our clients' businesses.

“We engineer modern business to improve everyday life.”

Shared purpose with Cognizant

Naming rules

Cognizant Moment

Always the full, trademarked name.

Initial caps in copy

The logo is lowercase, but writing always uses caps.

Never “Moment”

Never shorten or abbreviate the name.

Always a sub-brand

Present in relationship to Cognizant.

Logo

Files live in brand/logos/. Only two variants exist. Never modify the logo — not the wordmark, not the hexagon; don't separate, recolour, or recreate it. Pick the variant by background; scale by height only (ratio 200×47 ≈ 4.26:1); min ~24px tall; keep clear space around it.

02 · Brand voice & tone

Clear, inspiring, purposeful

Our tone complements the visual identity with a verbal one. No matter the audience, it must always be unmistakably Cognizant Moment.

PrincipleHow we speak✅ We say🚫 We don't say
01 Inviting, not intimidatingSpeak with people, not at them. Confident but never condescending.“Let's look at this another way.”“I don't think that will work.”
02 Fresh, not forcedAvoid jargon and corporate clichés. Intelligent, elegant, clear.“What if the real insight comes from our customers?”“Let's circle back after taking this offline with leadership.”
03 Warmly globalWorldly intelligence — open, inclusive, curious; not rooted in one culture.“There's always another perspective. Let's explore it.”“Here's the one-size-fits-all answer.”
04 Grounded, yet elevatedShow up with clarity, insight, and a touch of poetry. A conversation, not a performance.“Change doesn't have to be loud to be powerful.”“Let's disrupt the paradigm with radical innovation.”
05 Brave enough to be simpleGet to the point with grace. Speak plainly, but never dully.“What matters here is what people feel.”“Our proprietary framework optimizes emotional engagement.”

04 · Accessibility

Colour contrast

We follow WCAG AA — a contrast value of 4.5 or greater. Use only these tested pairings.

Works on / with white

Midnight blue Dark teal* Dark blue* Dark plum Medium plum* Dark gray Highlight red

Works on / with midnight blue

White Dark teal* Medium teal Light teal Medium plum Light plum Medium blue Light blue Dark blue* Medium gray Light gray Highlight yellow

* Use dark teal, medium plum and dark blue as text only when larger than 19px/14pt bold, or 24px/18pt regular.

06 · Components

Basic components

Buttons

And a link on dark

Filled = light teal + midnight text. Hollow = dark blue border/text (light teal on dark). Shape: pill · text: 20px.

Tabs

Active tab uses dark blue text with a dark-blue top indicator on white. Rounding: 0.5em.
Inactive tabs use a 20% tint of Accent 2 Light.
Tabs share a single rounded container.

Accordions

Background blends from midnight blue → dark blue down the stack (via :nth-of-type in components.css); white text; arrow-down icon.
Rounding: 0.5em (global radius).
Each item steps one tone lighter toward dark blue.
