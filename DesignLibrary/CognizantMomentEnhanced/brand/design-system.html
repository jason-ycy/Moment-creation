Notch Frame

Crops any image to a rectangle with its top-left and bottom-right corners cut diagonally — top-left cut straight from the top-right corner down to 40% of the left edge; bottom-left stays square; bottom edge flat to 60% before cutting up to 50% of the right edge. No fixed aspect ratio of its own; sized here by its container.

Notch Outline

A distinct shape from Notch Frame above — no fill, only a 1.5px gradient-stroked outline at 30% opacity (#003B50 → #D9D9D9). Each instance needs its own SVG gradient id since ids must be unique per document.
